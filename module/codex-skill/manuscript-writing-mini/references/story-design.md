# Scientific story before section names
For original research distinguish background, actual tension, gap, RQ, actual
methodological move, main finding, supporting finding, interpretation/mechanism,
boundary and significance. Identify decisive evidence, not the most attractive
metric. Use nature-writing's argument and paper-type playbooks; no invented causal
or mechanistic narrative. For reviews select scope, organizing principle,
taxonomy/comparison dimensions, agreement, disagreement, gaps and reasoned stance:
what new usable knowledge organization does this review provide? Avoid a paper-by-
paper report. Gate A confirms story and manuscript positioning, not future research.
