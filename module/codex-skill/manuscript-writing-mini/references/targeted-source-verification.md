# Targeted source verification
Use nature-reader's matching source-format extraction/locator guidance. Verify the
selected original figure/table/equation/number, exact panel/page (or honest structure
locator for HTML), original caption, adjacent text, authors' actual claim, what
shown, assumptions, context and reuse appropriateness. Do not trigger full bilingual
translation unless requested or context genuinely requires it. Record original path,
SHA-256, verification timestamp, read locator, observed facts, scientific boundary,
source mode, licence/permission/attribution and target brief hash. Old card summaries
or inventory thumbnails cannot mark VERIFIED. A changed original or changed brief
invalidates that record. For concrete numeric citations, complex methods, disputed
conclusions, limitations, critical gaps or strong review synthesis, re-read relevant
primary passages. Use bounded searches for explicit section/claim coverage gaps in
the writing plan, including missing comparisons and contrary evidence; do not restart
broad discovery. Verify newly used sources and update project-local references and
source records without changing the frozen input handoff.

Writing-stage literature numbers may use numeric_trace.csv source_mode=LITERATURE_NUMERIC without changing the frozen handoff. Include verification_path, verification_sha256 and citation_key. The VERIFIED source record must bind the original path/hash, exact locator, observed evidence, citation_key and numeric_values entries with matching result_id/value/unit/text_excerpt. Such rows cannot reuse handoff result IDs or satisfy a missing central experimental result. Source and verification files participate in the content fingerprint. This is mechanical linkage, not automatic verification of scientific truth.
