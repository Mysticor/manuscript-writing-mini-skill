# Production and provenance
Real photos/model outputs/simulations must use original assets, never generated
replacements. Allowed processing: honest crop/resize/global contrast correction,
standard annotations/arrows/ROI/labels/composition, with history and originals kept.
Do not alter features/measurements, remove inconvenient evidence or fabricate outputs.
Capture original asset/E IDs and exact source; mixed panels require component-level
provenance. AI image schematics are labelled DRAFT, not quantitative evidence.

Route approved figure briefs to nature-figure. Reuse its saved backend or task choice,
ask its one-time backend question only if unresolved, and keep its figure QA.
Stage 2 does not imply submission-readiness or actual journal policy clearance.
Retain editable sources/plotting code/source data and appropriate SVG/vector PDF
plus PNG preview. Tables retain CSV and readable final table plus caption. Figure
PDF outputs are figure assets, not full-manuscript LaTeX publication build.
manifest rows store asset ID/type/role/source type/asset IDs/E IDs/paper IDs,
processing/generator/code/provenance/rights/final path/status and checksum.
All required final outputs must exist; previews alone cannot prove visual correctness.
