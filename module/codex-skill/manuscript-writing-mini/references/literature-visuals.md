# Prepare early → decide late → verify at use
Use Stage 1 inventories to shortlist; do not rescan every PDF. Inventory is navigation.
Once a component enters the manuscript plan, create source_verification record and
reopen the exact original paper. Mode is one of:
REFERENCE_ONLY: understanding only; not a final embedded component.
LITERATURE_REPRODUCED: direct panel/image; paper, DOI, figure/panel/page, licence,
permission and attribution are required. Unknown permission blocks formal inclusion.
LITERATURE_ADAPTED: original structure redrawn; source figure, scientific information,
modifications and citation/permission basis recorded. Redrawing does not erase rights.
LITERATURE_SYNTHESIZED: new organization across papers; fact→source mapping and the
manuscript's new synthesis are explicit. Prefer synthesis for reviews when justified.
Formulas/tables follow the same verify-at-use rule. Interpretation-only reference
may become a used component only after a new verification and rights decision.
